```python
@gene("r3", "retreat on foot: below 30% health and under fire, unless an enemy champion in reach dies to our next basic attack (take the kill instead)")
def r_run() -> Bool:
    if at_spawn() or not hurt_below(0.3) or not under_fire():
        return False
    return not any(in_reach(u) and attack_damage(me(), u) >= u.health + shield(u) for u in enemies())
```
