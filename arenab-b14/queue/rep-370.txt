```python
@gene("m9", "spawn tiles of respawning groups, except groups back within 8 turns (worth walking to now: we arrive as they respawn)")
def respawning() -> Tiles:
    acc = camp_spawns() - camp_spawns()
    for g in range(num_groups()):
        if group_respawning(g) and group_cooldown(g) > 8:
            acc = acc | group_tiles(g)
    return acc
```
