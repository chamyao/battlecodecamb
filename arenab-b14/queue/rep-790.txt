```python
@gene('s6', 'visible enemy champions within r tiles that can chase us: not recalling, not channelling a tribute and not standing on their own spawn')
def enemies_within(r: Num) -> Num:
    p = here()
    es = enemy_spawn()
    return count(dist(p, u.pos) <= r and not is_recalling(u) and not is_tributing(u) and not (u.pos in es) for u in enemies())
```
