```python
@gene("a5", "cast-ahead applies: nearest enemy within 5 (6 for a gem carrier worth catching) and not on its spawn")
def ahead_on() -> Bool:
    f = ahead_foe()
    if not exists(f) or f.pos in enemy_spawn():
        return False
    d = dist(here(), f.pos)
    return d <= 5 or (d <= 6 and unit_gems(f) >= 10)
```
