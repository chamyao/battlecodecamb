```python
@gene('r3', 'retreat on foot: below 30% health and under fire or with a threat near; below 50% when two or more enemy champions are within 4 (outnumbered)', parents=['r3'])
def r_run() -> Bool:
    if at_spawn():
        return False
    if hurt_below(0.3):
        return under_fire() or threat_near()
    return hurt_below(0.5) and enemies_within(4) >= 2
```
