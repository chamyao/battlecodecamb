```python
@gene('s10', 'fewest turns until a visible enemy champion could hit us (1000 if none), with a 1-tile margin; recalling enemies and enemies on their own spawn are ignored')
def threat_eta() -> Num:
    h = here()
    es = enemy_spawn()
    return min((eta(max(0, dist(h, u.pos) - u.attack_range - 1.0), u.move_speed) for u in enemies() if not is_recalling(u) and not (u.pos in es)), default=1000)
```
