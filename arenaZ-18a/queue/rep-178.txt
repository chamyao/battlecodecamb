```python
@gene("Z153g", "how much closer a step onto t brings me on the walking field (no danger penalty), avoiding tiles next to a healthier enemy champion")
def raw_gain(t: Tile) -> Num:
    gain = at(goal_field(), me().pos) - at(goal_field(), t)
    if any(dist(u.pos, t) <= 1 and u.health > me().health for u in enemies()):
        return gain - 1
    return gain
```
