# Sensing blocks the bot would need: one per gap, ranked by cost

Following Cham's way of working, each gap in `docs/GAP_LIST.md` becomes an observation the bot can read, and FunSearch finds how to use it. Every block below is computable from what one champion sees on its turn: its own state, the champions, camps, altars and locks it can see, the map, and shared memory.

Costs are from the 50 v21 games against the +100 band (`docs/GAP_LIST.md`; ± is one bootstrap SE).

| rank | gap (cost per game) | "the bot would need to observe X to avoid this" | proposed block(s) | notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Gems lost on death**: 184 ± 23 gems. 54% of champion deaths are while carrying, at a median of 7 tiles from an altar. | **Whether I can reach an altar before a visible enemy can reach me**, given my bag. | `altar_eta()`: turns to the nearest open (unlocked) altar-adjacent tile, by path. `threat_eta()`: the fewest turns until any visible enemy champion could hit me (path distance minus its attack range, movement 1 per turn, +2 if its dash slot could be ready; Chebyshev bound when there is no path). `enemy_between(Tile)`: whether a visible enemy is within 3 of the straight path from me to the tile. | `threat_eta() < altar_eta()` while carrying is the situation that loses the gems. Also used by rank 3. |
| 2 | **Low capture yield**: 10.9 gems per capture against their 14.9, so we capture 815 against their 895 per game (712 against 973 in our losses). | **The gems-per-turn value of a camp group, including travel and kill time**, and whether someone else is closer. | `group_rate(Tile)` = group gems ÷ (path turns from me + group HP ÷ my damage per turn + the capture timer). `group_contested(Tile)`: the number of visible enemies closer to the group than me. | Composable today from `group_gems`, `group_hp`, `distance` and `strength()`, but a direct block makes the comparison searchable in one step. |
| 3 | **Too few bank trips**: 18 against their 26 tributes per game (14 against 30 in our losses). Gems held longer get lost (rank 1). | **The expected cost of carrying on**: my bag × the chance of contact before I bank. | Uses `altar_eta()` and `threat_eta()`, plus `bag_at_risk()` = gems() if `threat_eta()` ≤ `altar_eta()`, else 0. Also `team_bag()`: the sum of allies' carried gems, from shared memory. | Lets the search learn a bank threshold that depends on danger instead of a fixed 25. |
| 4 | **Deaths to camps**: 4.2 ± 0.7 per game, about 40 gems and 60 rounds of absence each. | **Whether I can finish this camp group before it kills me.** | `group_dps(Tile)`: the summed damage per turn of the group's living camps at their design values. `survive_turns(Tile)` = health() ÷ `group_dps`, compared with group HP ÷ my damage per turn. | `group_threat` gives summed strength but not the race against my HP. |
| 5 | **Dying after dropping to half HP**: 45% of 24 crossings per game die, against 19% for the band. The risk is set at the first hit: outnumbered → 57% vs 44%; facing a champion with 200+ more max HP → 53% vs 41%. | **Whether this fight is winnable before it starts**: numbers and the size of the nearest enemy. Also whether I can get out (turns until they can reach me, against the recall time). | Already added: `enemies_near` / `allies_near` / `strength_at(Tile, r)` and `unit_max_health` / `unit_strength` / `unit_focus`. New: `recall_safe()` = `threat_eta()` > recall turns (5, or 3 with Home). `retreat_eta()`: path turns to my spawn. | 5% of crossings die mid-recall and 13% while heading home, so `recall_safe` and `retreat_eta` vs `threat_eta` cover the escape side. |
| 6 | **Item spread vs carry stacking.** Team-mean stats are level, but their killers carry 49 vs 20 item points over our victims after round 400 (strength +90). | **Whether a visible enemy is a stacked carry, and whether our own items are concentrated.** | `unit_item_points(Unit)`: estimated from the unit's visible stats against its design base, e.g. (strength − base) ÷ growth + (max HP − base) ÷ growth. `team_max_strength()`: the highest strength among visible allies and me, or from shared memory. | With the new `unit_strength`, a carry is `unit_strength(u)` well above its champion's base; a block that normalises by champion type saves the search from learning every base value. |
| 7 | **Killers out of vision**: 35% of our decisive deaths against the band (26-33% against the top 9) came from a champion unseen for 3 rounds. | **Enemies I can't see now but saw recently nearby.** | `unseen_enemies_near(r, age)`: enemy champions whose last-seen position (kept in per-bot memory) is within r of me, seen at most `age` turns ago. `last_seen_count()`: the number of enemy champions seen in the last 10 turns. | Needs per-champion memory of sightings; bot memory or shared bytes. |
| 8 | **Farming camps with enemies near**: 15% of deaths within 5 rounds of a camp attack. | Covered by `enemies_near` and `threat_eta()`. | none new | |
| 9 | **Dying outnumbered**: 62% of deaths. Not a separator against the band (74% in our wins). | Covered by `enemies_near` / `allies_near` / `strength_at`. | none new | |

Suggested sim metrics per block, so the lines can check a block is used well while holding win rate against v21:
- Ranks 1 and 3: `gems_lost_to_champs` (interp_eval `--details`).
- Rank 2: captured gems per capture. Needs a `--details` field.
- Rank 4: `deaths[us] − kills[them]`.
- Rank 5: the death rate after a low-HP crossing. Needs per-round HP, or an `@@EV` death event carrying the victim's HP 15 rounds earlier.
- Rank 6: max strength over our champions at rounds 400/600.
- Rank 7: the share of deaths whose killer was not visible to the victim 3 turns before.
