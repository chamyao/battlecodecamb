# Engine findings (cambc 3.0.5)

Facts established against the official binary. Where they differ from the
handbook text, **the binary wins**. Each entry says how it was established.

Oracle tooling lives in `oracle/` (`setup_workspace.sh` builds a cambc
workspace; bots in `oracle/bots/`; parsers in `oracle/tools/`).

## Engine constants

Source: `oracle/bots/dump` reads the raw ABI records (`game_settings`,
`champion_design`, `camp_design`, `item_info`, `consumable_info`).
Decoded copy: `oracle/fixtures/engine_constants.json` (identical on all 40 maps).

Game settings: capture 5, camp sleep 2, respawn 60, recall 5, tribute
duration `max(1, ceil(0.1 * gems))` (base 0), tribute range 1 (Chebyshev),
max rounds 2000, winning gems 1000, kill gold 100, **assist gold 0**,
**passive gold 0**, spawn healing 1.0 (fraction of max health per heal),
1 active ward, 1 ward charge, ward cooldown 10, ward lifetime -1 (permanent),
ward vision radius 6, ward placement radius 2, altar lock 5 hits, teleport
crystal channel 3, shared memory 8 B, draft memory 1024 B,
tile reveal 1 round.

Every champion: vision radius 8, move speed 1, camp damage multiplier 1.0.
Stats match the handbook tables.

Ability records (kind, range, radius, modifier, duration, flags). Flag
`attributes & 1` = "exclusive with movement". Target bits: 1 camps,
2 enemies, 4 allies, 8 self, 16 ground.

Treasure 0.2, Jungle 0.3, Home 0.5.

Consumables: raw ids 0 = teleport crystal, 2 = altar lock (cost 10, carry 1);
ids 1 and 3 exist with carry limit 0 (retired).

## Map file format (.map26a)

Reverse-engineered; `oracle/tools/map26a.py` decodes all 40 pool maps to
exactly the engine's own masks, spawns, camp handles and camp groups.
See the module docstring for the byte layout. Key points:

- Terrain layers are run-length coded over the grid padded to 8x8 chunks,
  in engine chunk order.
- Each bush tile carries a bush-cluster id (stored map).
- Only one half of the camps is stored; the other half is generated by the
  map symmetry. Camp handles are assigned in file order, each camp followed
  by its mirror; a camp on the symmetry axis/centre has no twin.
- Spawn B is the mirror of spawn A.
- Camp groups: cardinal (4-neighbour) flood fill, visiting camps in handle
  order; group ids in order of first handle; members in handle order;
  rewards are the sum of member rewards; respawn timer is the max.

## Setup and identities

- Champion ids: team A's champions are 0..n-1 in pick order, then team B's.
  (Cassi & Issac id layout: not yet verified.)
- Initial positions fill the spawn box row-major (x fastest) in id order.

## Geometry primitives and vision

Source: `oracle/bots/geom` fuzzes the engine's own host functions on random
wall masks and real maps (fixtures in `oracle/fixtures/geom/`). All of the
following match the engine exactly on every sample (thousands per primitive).

- **Shadowcast** (`Bitboard::shadow_cast`): Albert Ford's symmetric
  shadowcasting (four quadrants, slopes `(2col-1)/2depth`, round-ties-up /
  round-ties-down column bounds), limited to **Chebyshev** radius `dist`
  (rows deeper than `dist` are not scanned). A tile is revealed only if it is
  symmetric-visible **and not blocking**: walls themselves are never marked
  visible. The origin is always visible. Out-of-map tiles do not block.
- **Champion vision**: shadowcast radius 8 from the champion's tile, blocking
  set = walls + bushes not in the viewer's own bush cluster (cluster ids come
  from the map file). So bush tiles of other clusters are never visible, and
  one's own cluster is transparent (and visible where in line of sight).
- **Ward vision**: same rule with radius 6, using the ward tile's bush
  cluster.
- **Reachable tiles**: plain 8-neighbour BFS up to `dist` steps over
  non-wall tiles; diagonal steps may cut corners. If the origin is a wall the
  result is empty.
- **Distance fields**: multi-source 8-neighbour BFS from non-wall roots
  (roots on walls are dropped), walls impassable, stops when the next depth
  would exceed `max_distance`; `compute_to` stops before expanding once any
  destination has a distance. Unreached tiles hold 0xFFFF. `visited` = tiles
  that received a distance. The expansion count is the number of BFS
  iterations executed (including the final one that finds no new tiles).
- **draw_circle**: tiles with `dx^2 + dy^2 <= r^2`; **draw_chebyshev**:
  `max(|dx|,|dy|) <= r` (fractional radii allowed).

## Rules established by differential replay

Source: `oracle/bots/tracer` (seeded semi-random policy exercising every action,
logging full observations at each turn start/end and every raw call's result)
replayed through the simulator by `sim/build/replay` (`oracle/run_trace.py`).
Each rule below was needed to make full games replay with zero differences.

### Turn structure and order
- Rounds start at 1. Team A acts first in odd rounds, team B in even rounds;
  the teams alternate turn by turn (A1 B1 A2 B2 ...).
- Within a team: higher priority first, ties by champion id. `set_priority`
  takes effect at the next round start.
- Per-turn allowances (moved/attacked/cast flags) reset for *every* champion
  at round start, so an ally that has not acted yet shows fresh allowances; after
  its turn the flags persist (plus `completed_turn`) until the next round.
  The `recall_started`/`tribute_started`/`*_cancelled` bits clear at turn end.
- Champion turn start: respawn (if timer done), ward recharge (also while
  dead), expire zero-duration buffs, then complete channels whose timer is 0.
  Turn end: decrement cooldowns, buffs, channel timer, ward cooldown and (when
  dead) respawn timer; then spawn healing (full heal on own spawn).
- The game can end at a turn *start* (a tribute completing); the engine then
  stops before that bot runs. `roundsCompleted` is then the previous round.

### Spawning and placement
- Team A's champions take spawn-box slots in row-major order (x fastest);
  team B's slot *i* is the mirror image of team A's slot *i* under the map
  symmetry. Recall and respawn land on the first free slot in that order;
  tiles reserved by a teleport crystal count as occupied.

### Camps
- Camp turns happen at round start (after respawns and captures).
  A camp targets the **nearest champion by Chebyshev distance** within its
  attack range; ties go to the champion earliest in **this round's turn
  order**. Camps ignore walls/bushes for targeting.
- Sleep is a timer: set to `camp_sleep_time` (2) when the camp is woken and
  whenever a champion is in range at its turn (even while stunned); decremented
  at the end of every camp turn. An awake camp with no champion in range and
  a zero timer falls asleep (keeping its health), **even while stunned**.
- Any damage to a camp wakes every sleeping member of its group (and sets
  their timers to 2); it does **not** refresh already-awake camps' timers.
- Stunned camps do not attack. Buff timers: expire at
  camp turn start when 0, decrement at camp turn end (a 1-turn stun applied
  during the champion phase blocks the next camp turn).
- Gem/gold clusters (strength 0) never deal damage but do keep themselves
  awake by having champions in range (range 3).

### Capture
- When all camps of a group are dead the group becomes "reward available".
  At each capture phase (round start): if exactly one team occupies any of the
  group's spawn tiles, a timer starts at 5 (`capture_time`) or counts down; on
  reaching 0 the reward is paid and the group starts respawning. Progress
  resets **the moment** the region becomes empty or contested, checked after
  every position change (walks, dashes, recalls, teleports, deaths), not only
  at capture phases.
- Rewards are split equally among the capturing team's champions standing on
  the region at completion; the lowest id gets the remainder.
- Group respawn is delayed while any of its spawn tiles holds a champion **or
  is the destination of an in-flight teleport** (wards on the tiles do not
  block).

### Gold
- Gold is stored **exactly in hundredths** (fixed point); bonus multipliers
  (Vesper Vale +33%, Treasure +20%, additive) give `amount * (100 + pct)`
  hundredths, and queries report `floor(gold)`. E.g. 36 base gold gives 47.88
  (reported 47); the fraction carries into later gains. Float or double
  storage both drift by 1 gold over long games (00rows seed 11: 27.30 + 137.70
  must read 165).

### Combat
- Basic attack damage = strength, then attacker modifiers applied
  sequentially in float: Heart of Ruin `x(1 + 0.5 * (1 - hp/max_hp))`, then
  Monster emblem `x1.1` vs camps (order matters for float rounding).
- Varg's splash hits other enemy champions and camps within Chebyshev 1 of
  the primary target; splash damage does not trigger Ren's retaliation.
- Ren's retaliation (0.2 x focus) triggers on any direct hit, including
  zero-damage hits such as Cassi's stun, and against camps; retaliation damage
  does not itself trigger retaliation. Ren's outgoing modifiers apply to it
  (Monster emblem x1.1 against camps).
- Ground-targeted abilities (AoE, dash) cast with a unit target use the unit's
  tile as the destination.
- Dash path: the destination is validated like a landing tile; a 2-step dash
  needs an intermediate tile that is not a wall or an enemy spawn tile and
  holds no camp or enemy champion **visible to the caster** (allies and unseen
  enemies, e.g. in a bush, can be passed; holes can be crossed). No path ->
  error 32. (Enemy spawn: 01elk-arena round 630, tune-1 as team B, Vesper Vale
  at (14,27) dashing to (16,25), whose only intermediate (15,26) is in team A's
  spawn box: cambc 32, the simulator had let it through.)
- Attack resets require that the basic attack was already used this turn
  (otherwise 26); for ranged champions a reset restores the attack only, not
  the walk.
- Death resets ability cooldowns immediately; the ward cooldown keeps ticking.

### Stun
- A stunned champion's restriction mask is 0x3f (move, attack, cast, shop,
  consumable, ward) and its `can_*` state bits clear. Errors while stunned:
  move 21, attack 40, cast 26, consumable 33, ward 63, buy 33, tribute 45.

### Vision-dependent queries
- Allies need vision just like enemies (E97 otherwise). A dead champion sees
  only through its ward.
- Wards are listed (both teams) when the ward's tile is in the viewer's
  vision, in placement order; re-placing a ward keeps its list slot.
- Teleport reservations are listed when the destination is visible or the
  viewer is the caster; `channel_type` is 4.
- Camp-group state (`camp_group_state`): alive/reward states need a visible
  group tile; a champion that has seen a group respawning (including at the
  moment it was captured) keeps getting state 3 while the respawn timer is
  above 0. Once the timer reaches 0 but the group is still blocked, only
  champions currently seeing a group tile get state 3.

### Altars, locks, tribute
- Each team may hold one lock per altar (both teams can lock the same altar).
- Placing a lock cancels every tribute channel at that altar, allies included.
- A tribute at an enemy-locked altar is converted into a basic attack on the
  lock when an attack is possible (returns success); otherwise error 125.

### Validation order (first failing check wins)
Established from normal traces plus fuzzed traces (tracer seeds >= 100 issue
random actions with random arguments while alive, dead, stunned and
channelling). "Incapacitated" = dead or stunned: a dead champion gets the
same per-action code as a stunned one (shopping aside, which the dead may do).
- move: incapacitated 21 -> channel (34/35/123) -> already moved 36 (a ranged
  champion's attack or attack reset also counts, 36) -> destination legality 22 -> reach 38 (no 37 observed). Hidden
  occupant: ok=0, tile revealed for the round.
- attack: incapacitated 40 -> channel -> target validity 42 -> already
  attacked 41 (a ranged champion's move counts as its attack, also 41) ->
  range 43. An attack reset after a ranged move restores the attack.
- cast: incapacitated 26 -> channel -> slot/ability -> cooldown 28 -> already
  cast 27 -> attack-reset precondition 26 (needs a spent attack; a ranged
  champion's move spends it) -> moved/exclusive 126 -> targeting. Unit
  targets on ground abilities: an AoE uses the tile of a visible living enemy
  champion or camp, anything else (ally, dead, unseen, bad handle) is 30; a
  dash ignores the unit target entirely and uses the given destination (so
  (-1,-1) gives 32). Dash intermediate tiles are also blocked by an in-flight
  teleport destination.
- buy: stun 33 -> channel -> not on spawn 49 -> invalid/unpurchasable 50 ->
  count 51 -> gold 52.
- buy consumable: channel (34/35/123) -> stun 33 -> not on spawn 55 ->
  invalid id 56 (outside 0..3) -> carry limit 58 (ids 1 and 3 have limit 0, so
  they always give 58) -> gold 59.
- sell (Tavi): channel -> stun 33 -> not Tavi 128 -> invalid -> not owned 130
  -> not on spawn 133 -> already sold 131.
- use consumable: incapacitated 33 -> channel -> invalid type 60 -> not owned
  61 -> lock / teleport checks.
- recall: incapacitated 44 -> channel (34/35/123, recalling again is 34) ->
  already started this turn 113.
- cancel: not channelling 115 -> teleport 124 -> already cancelled 116, counted
  per channel type (a tribute cancel does not use up the recall cancel).
- tribute: dead 45 -> already started 114 -> channel -> enemy lock on the tile
  (range 48, then converted to an attack on the lock, else 125) -> stunned 45
  -> no gems 46 -> bad altar 47 -> range 48.
- ward: incapacitated 63 -> channel -> no charge 62 -> bad tile 64 (out of
  map, wall, hole, altar, **enemy spawn zone**) -> range 65 -> any allied ward
  (own included) on tile 117 if the tile is visible; an allied ward on a tile
  the champion cannot see acts like a hidden occupant (rc 0, nothing placed,
  the tile is revealed).
- teleport crystal: validated like a move (bad destination 22, beyond 3 tiles
  37).

## Rule coverage

`sim/build/replay_cov` is the replayer built with `CBS_RULE_COVERAGE`: it
counts every time a replayed game reaches a situation decided by a rule that
was first inferred rather than observed (`CBS_RULE_HIT` in the code). Over
124 traced games (seeds 11-13 on all 40 maps, 00elkring seeds 1-4), all with
zero diffs, these were reached and are therefore confirmed:

| Rule | Hits (games) |
| --- | --- |
| Any channel blocks shopping, even on spawn | 14144 (124) |
| Camps keep their health when they fall asleep | 15522 (124) |
| Teleport crystal beyond 3 tiles -> error 37 (`move_out_of_range`) | 3224 (124) |
| Durability (x0.9) is applied before Jungle (x0.7) on camp hits | 10012 (58) |
| Health-to-strength passive: `strength += (max_hp - base_hp) * 0.01` in float | 3303 (75) |
| Re-stunning keeps the longer remaining duration (camps) | 211 (92) |
| Re-stunning keeps the longer remaining duration (champions) | 35 (10) |
| Stunned recall -> error 44 (`recall_cant_channel`) | 202 (59) |
| A second draft-memory submission overwrites the prefix (size = max) | 872 (124) |
| Enhanced-attack buff lasts only for the casting turn | 7 (6) |
| Ren's retaliation does not trigger another Ren's retaliation | 1 (1) |

Never reached (still `RULE?`): the engine's random pick for a bot that does
not draft (our fill uses our own RNG and cannot match), a teleport whose
destination is occupied at completion, action calls by a dead champion that
reach the common checks, stunned purchase of an invalid consumable (order of
the two errors), Tavi selling while stunned and channelling, a dash past a
camp the caster cannot see, and whether a dash may pass over an altar (29
dashes had an altar among the candidate intermediate tiles and replayed
correctly treating altars as passable, but the counter does not show whether
the altar was the only path).

## Match seeding
- `cambc run` has no seed option, and reruns of the same two bots on the same map produce
  the same `match.seed` and the same game. For more samples vary the map, the side or the
  bots' own seeds.
- The simulator reproduces whole cambc games of the deploy bot exactly when its policy players
  are seeded as the bot seeds itself (`interp_eval --bot-seeds`; bot/src/main.cpp:
  `0x5eed0000 + team*64 + champ + 1` per instance). v5 vs v4 (deploy/v0 b39ac4d) on all 40 maps,
  both sides: 80/80 games identical in winner, both teams' tributed gems and end round. Note
  that cambc's `roundsCompleted` is one less than the round the game ended in, when it ended
  mid-round. Policy RNG was the only source of divergence. The engine's own randomness
  (`match.seed`) did not show up in these games: the draft random fill is never reached when
  every bot picks.
- Consequence: with the bot's fixed seeds, `random_champion` makes the same choices in every
  game, so a near-mirror always drafts the same two compositions (v4/v5: A Vesper Vale, Tavi,
  Marcella, Stellar, Ren; B Vesper Vale, Willow, Ryth, Stellar, Heart of Ruin, truncated on
  maps with fewer picks). That composition, not the side, is cambc's "A advantage". v4 mirror:
  team A wins 60/80 with bot seeds (identical to cambc) against 199/400 with per-game seeds.
  cambc's 80 games are 80 samples of one draft per pick count; per-game seeds sample drafts.

## Real ladder games (differential replay)

The 50 unranked games of tune-5 against the ladder's top 9 and vc? (24 Oct, `analysis/sprint1`
`data/top-teams-2026-10-24`) were replayed through the simulator with `oracle/replays/`: both
teams' recorded inputs, full state compared at every champion turn start. **41/50 replay end
to end with zero differences** (353k of 414k champion turns compared, same end round and
score), with real fights: kills, HP after every hit, stuns, dashes, Heart of Ruin, Marcella,
Prism, Ryth, Vendetta and Vesper Vale abilities, shopping and altar-lock attacks.

Open: the other 9 games stop at a move the engine rejects but the simulator allows. In all
112 such rejections (50 games) the target tile holds an **allied ward that is not the mover's
own**, the tile is a bush and neither the mover nor its own ward sees it; enemy wards never
block (130 moves onto them succeed). But 369 moves in exactly that situation succeed, often
the same move onto the same ward in another round (01yellow-brick-road: (17,12)->(16,11)
blocked in round 35, allowed in round 74, where the mover's own ward also saw the tile). Not
separated by: ward age, owner alive or position, turn order, team, step direction, the
mover's earlier actions or earlier blocks, the owner's or the ward's view. Every simple rule
tried (allied/any ward occupies; unseen allied/any ward is a hidden occupant; visible allied
ward is occupied) breaks more games than it fixes, so the simulator keeps wards passable.
`oracle/replays/wardmoves.js` lists the cases.
