This run FITS an opponent to real data: the score is how closely this policy's games against several versions of
our bot reproduce the statistics the ladder's top teams actually recorded against those same versions (win rate,
gems both sides, champion kills both sides, champion damage both sides, first-kill round, and the share of our
bot's deaths that came while it was outnumbered). It is NOT scored on winning by as much as possible: a version
that crushes our bot harder than the real teams did fits worse, just as one that is too weak. Change behaviour,
not numbers alone: when to engage, with how many allies, which target, ability timing, what to buy, when to bank.
Top teams farm rich groups, bank 40-60 gem bags when safe, fight in pairs (2v1), open with stuns/dashes at their
range, cast resets after a basic attack, and keep ranged champions at range 3.
Fuel rule: every dash or host search along a long path (dash_towards, move_towards to far tiles, closest on big
sets) sits behind a fuel_remaining() gate (e.g. `if fuel_remaining() > 55000:`), or it can hit the 100k cap.
