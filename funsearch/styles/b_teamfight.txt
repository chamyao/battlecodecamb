This run explores the TEAM FIGHT style, which none of our policies play yet. Our champions
currently wander alone and lose even 1v1 fights. Develop a policy whose champions act as a
group:
- move together: commit to the same camp group with claim(tile) / claim_count(tile) (camp
  claims are shared through memory), and wait for or walk toward allies (allies_visible(),
  allies_within(r)) before engaging; regroup when isolated;
- focus fire: pick one enemy (weakest, or a gem carrier via richest()/unit_gems) and name it
  with set_team_target(unit); every champion that can reach team_target() attacks it and casts
  its damage/stun abilities at it (slot_is_damage, slot_is_stun, slot_is_aoe);
- contest the enemy's camps: go as a group to rich camps (gem clusters, elk groups) near the
  enemy side or where enemies are seen, and fight there with the numbers advantage;
- take fights with the numbers on your side: enemies_within(r) counts enemy champions near
  you and local_strength(r) compares your group's health x damage with theirs (pair it with
  enemies_within(r) > 0); bare retreat/chase thresholds on it lost in our tests, so use it to
  decide WHERE and WITH WHOM to fight (wait for allies, pull back toward them), not only
  whether. Name every threshold as a constant.
Keep farming, tributing and recall rules that already work. Every version must keep using
team coordination (set_team_target/team_target and claim/claim_count); a version that drops
the style is rejected.
Our main ladder loss is to melee stun/dive teams that kill us 21-34 times a game while our
ranged champions trade in place. A group fight answers this: ranged champions kite (if
i_am_ranged() and a melee enemy is adjacent, move_away_from it toward allies, then attack from
range) while allies converge on the diver and focus it down.
Camp choice matters in every style: many of our deaths are to camps we cannot clear. Use
group_threat(g), group_hp(g) and group_gems(...) to choose camps by value against danger and
distance: skip a goal g when group_threat(g) * group_hp(g) / (strength() * (1 + allies_within(4)))
> health() (that is, the damage taken while clearing it exceeds what you can absorb), and
otherwise prefer groups with more gems per turn of walking and clearing.
