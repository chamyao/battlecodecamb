This run explores the LATE MONOLITH style, which none of our policies play yet. On the ladder
the team capturing more monoliths wins ~80% of games where they exist; top teams take them with
3-4 champions after about round 1,000 (success 0% solo, 29% with 2, 49% with 3, 69% with 4),
commit ~30 rounds, recall individually at low health and come back, and abandon below 2
attackers. A monolith has 20k hp, 200 strength (range 5) and gives 200 gems and 300 gold, and
must be captured by standing on its spawn tile for 5 rounds after it dies.
Develop a policy that:
- builds up first: farms camps and buys damage/health (e.g. Bruiser/Power strength, Jungle for
  less camp damage) until a start condition (round number, gold or items bought, number of
  allies alive) — name it as a constant;
- then sends 3-4 champions together to the same monolith (coordinate with claim(tile) and
  claim_count(tile); wait at a safe distance outside its range 5 until enough allies arrive),
  every attacker hitting it and casting abilities each turn;
- each champion recalls on its own at low health (recall is cancelled by damage: step out of
  range 5 first), heals on spawn, and returns; abandon when fewer than 2 attackers remain;
- after the kill, stands on the capturable spawn to capture, then tributes the 200 gems
  (prefer altars outside ring(monolith_tiles(), 5)).
A plain "go to the monolith after round 1,000" rule lost badly in our tests, so the build-up,
grouping and sustain matter. On maps without monoliths (count(monolith_tiles()) == 0) play
normally. Every version must keep the monolith plan; a version that drops it is rejected.
