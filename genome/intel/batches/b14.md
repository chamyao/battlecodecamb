# Batch 14: live v38 on engine 2.0.0, with 40% new maps

10 unranked groups were queued at 03:00Z on 2026-10-30, against the same 10 opponents as b13. Quota before queueing: 10 of 50 used.

**Design** (b14_plan.json):
- Each opponent keeps b13's first 3 maps and sides: 30 games paired with b13. Same bot (v38) and same engine, so differences there are noise or opponent changes.
- The last 2 slots go to 20 of the 30 maps new in the 2.0.0 pool (seeded shuffle, seed 14).
- The 10 new maps left over are for b15: 00vault, adgato_gemcombat3, 00the_line, 00ribs, 02gift_wrapping, 02tangle, 02uncharted_lands, 02the_pond, 02stalactites, 00dunes.

## Results (all 50 finished; extracted with tools/extract_v2)

**Record: 24-26.** Our bot faults: 0. Full table in b14_report.md.

**Old-map part, paired with b13** (30 games, same bot and engine): 19-11 vs b13's 18-12, with 1 flip.
- Gems +13.8 ± 15.8; deaths to champions -0.4 ± 0.3; every other metric is within noise.
- Platform games of the same bot against the same opponent are close to deterministic: 29 of 30 games had the same result. So a paired band on the old maps detects small changes.

**New-map part** (20 games on 20 new maps): **5-15.**

- Loss causes on new maps: low-yield 10 of 15, fights 9 of 15.
- Wins: adgato_gemcombat2, 02arena, 02guardians, 00galaxy, 00breadcrumbs.

**Bug: v38 freezes on 00quarry.**
- The ladder game vs Tozo's ended 0-1000. Our team made 1 capture and 0 gems in 2000 rounds.
- Reproduced in real cambc 4.0.0 vs starter-cpp:
  - v38 (~/ws/bots/genome-a93635): 0 gems, 60 gold in total, no crashes.
  - v33 (~/ws/bots/genome-bossbush), same map and opponent: wins 1012-0.
  - So it is a v38 regression.
- From the replay (extract_v2):
  - The four champions clear the west gold clusters and capture once (round 11).
  - After that they stand at (6,9), (5,9), (5,10) and (5,11) for the rest of the game. (5,10) is a gold-cluster spawn tile, and an occupied spawn tile keeps the whole group from respawning.
  - At round 61 they kill the north group (4..6,7) from where they stand, but never step onto it to capture.
  - After round 63 they take no action at all.
- **02the-pack:** the ladder game was 0-0 (lost on gold tiebreak). Locally v38, v33 and the starter all score 0 gems there, so it is not v38-specific. It needs its own look.

**Implications:**
- New maps are about 43% of the ladder pool (30 of 70), and v38 goes 5-15 on them. The new-map half is now the band's weak part.
- The 00quarry deadlock is a concrete, testable bug.
- The new-map part (b14 rows with a new map) is the baseline for the next candidate's new-map games.
