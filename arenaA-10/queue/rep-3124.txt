```python
@gene('r3', 'retreat on foot: below 30% health and under fire, or below 50% under fire while carrying 15+ gems (a death hands the bag to the killer); a threat merely near no longer triggers it (screened: retreating on threat_near alone costs many games vs v33)')
def r_run() -> Bool:
    if at_spawn() or not under_fire():
        return False
    return hurt_below(0.3) or (gems() >= 15 and hurt_below(0.5))
```
