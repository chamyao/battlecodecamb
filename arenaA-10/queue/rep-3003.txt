@gene('w6', 'how much closer a step onto t brings me to GOAL (0: no walk this turn); turn-start field and blocks; below half health, never a step next to two or more enemy champions (a pinch), and below a quarter health never next to any')
def step_gain(t: Tile) -> Num:
    if not OPEN or t in BLOCK:
        return 0
    n = count(dist(u.pos, t) <= 1 for u in enemies())
    if (hurt_below(0.5) and n >= 2) or (hurt_below(0.25) and n >= 1):
        return 0
    return at(FIELD, here()) - at(FIELD, t)
