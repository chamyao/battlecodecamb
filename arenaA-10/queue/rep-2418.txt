```python
@gene('m5', 'tiles next to an altar that can be stood on (no walls, holes or altars), leaving out camp spawns (a respawn would take them) unless every one is a camp spawn')
def altar_ring() -> Tiles:
    r = ring(altars(), 1) - walls() - holes() - altars()
    free = r - camp_spawns()
    return free if size(free) > 0 else r
```
