```python
@gene('s4', 'how much closer a step brings me to the goals (cached field); carrying gems, a step into the attack reach of an enemy champion counts as no progress when hurt or when two or more champions could hit it')
def progress(t: Tile) -> Num:
    d = at(GF, me().pos) - at(GF, t)
    if d > 0 and gems() > 0:
        n = count(u.is_champion and dist(u.pos, t) <= max(u.attack_range, 1) for u in enemies())
        if n >= 2 or (n > 0 and me().health_frac < 0.5):
            return 0
    return d
```
